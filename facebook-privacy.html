Privacy policy for the Bulldogs Shorts Facebook app

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Bulldogs Shorts is a personal tool used only by its owner, Lewis Clothier, to schedule the Team Bulldogs Facebook Page's own short videos (Reels). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

  • The Team Bulldogs Facebook Page: using the Page permissions granted by the owner, it uploads the club's own videos as Reels, schedules them, checks that they processed, and deletes scheduled Reels that are no longer needed.

What it does not do

  • It does not use Facebook Login for anyone other than the owner.
  • It does not read, collect or store personal data about Facebook users, such as profiles, friends, messages, comments or reactions.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties, or use it for advertising.

Storage

The only Facebook data kept is the Page access token, stored privately as an encrypted secret, and the IDs of the Reels it scheduled, kept in the owner's own schedule file. The owner can revoke access at any time in Facebook under Settings & privacy > Settings > Business integrations.
