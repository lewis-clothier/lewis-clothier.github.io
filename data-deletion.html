Data deletion instructions

Bulldogs Shorts does not collect or store personal data about Facebook users. The only data it keeps is the Team Bulldogs Page's access token and the IDs of the club's own scheduled Reels.

To remove the app's access

  1. In Facebook, open Settings & privacy > Settings.
  2. Go to Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  3. Find Bulldogs Shorts and click Remove.

This immediately stops the app from accessing the Page. To have any remaining stored token or Reel IDs deleted, email lewis.clothier@hotmail.co.uk and they will be deleted within 30 days.
