Privacy policy

Bulldogs Shorts is a personal tool used only by its owner, Lewis Clothier, to schedule the Bulldogs club's own videos.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive: reads the owner's content plan and video files, and writes a schedule, a log and video copies to a folder in the owner's own Drive.
  • YouTube: uploads the owner's videos to the owner's own channel and sets their publish date.

What it does with data

Data is used only to schedule the owner's own posts. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored privately as encrypted secrets and can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps & services).

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
